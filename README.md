## The image

A photorealistic aerial view of the Kerch Bridge across the strait between
Russia and occupied Crimea, moments after the defining world news event of
Tuesday 17 November 2026: a Ukrainian drone-and-missile strike brought down
the bridge's main road arch and a whole road span, dropping twisted steel and
concrete into the sea and cutting Russia's only direct road link to Crimea.
Smoke rises from the wreckage, debris drifts on the water, attack drones
circle in the overcast sky, and fires burn on the far shore. One scene, one
story: the 19-kilometre bridge that was Vladimir Putin's flagship link to
Crimea — destroyed in what President Volodymyr Zelenskyy called an
"unprecedented operation", the seventh stage of a weeks-long campaign to wear
down Russian air defences, carried out with domestically produced drones and
missiles in retaliation for Russia's strikes on Ukrainian infrastructure.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13) simply copies the image held in this repo to
`/output/entry.png`. No network, no randomness, no timestamps — running it
twice produces byte-identical output.
